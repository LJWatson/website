Help

Logging in

There are no passwords. Enter your email address on the log-in page and you'll get an email with a log-in link and a 6-digit code. Use either one. They work once, and stop working after 15 minutes.

Opening the link shows a Log in button. Select it to finish logging in.

Staying logged in

You stay logged in on each browser until you log out, even after closing the browser or restarting your device. Each device and browser needs its own log-in. Your Account page lists where you're logged in, and lets you log out of any of them.

Adding the app to an iPhone or iPad home screen

  1. Log in using Safari.
  2. Add the page to your home screen from Safari's Share menu.

The home screen app keeps you logged in. If you're ever logged out in the app, request a log-in email from the app, then type the code from the email into the app. The link in the email opens in Safari, not the app, so use the code instead.

Your lists

Make as many lists as you like on My lists, such as one for your birthday and one for Christmas. Every member can see your lists, apart from any you archive. Archiving hides a list without deleting it, and you can unarchive it at any time. Once a list's occasion date has passed, you'll be asked whether to archive it.

Use the Move up and Move down buttons to put the things you'd most like near the top.

Reserving gifts

On someone else's list, select Reserve to claim an item so no one else buys it too. Other members can see that you've reserved it. The list's owner never can, anywhere in the app.

Everything you've reserved is on My reservations, grouped by who it's for. Select Bought once you have something. Only you can see that: other members just see that you've reserved it. Cancel a reservation to let someone else reserve the item. If the owner changes an item after you reserve it, you'll see "Changed since you reserved it". If they delete it, you'll see that it was removed from their list.

Sharing a browser

If you use a browser where someone else is logged in, you'll see what they see, including who has reserved what. If someone has a list of their own, don't let them use your browser while you're logged in.

Problems

If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder. If you still can't log in, contact the person who invited you.
